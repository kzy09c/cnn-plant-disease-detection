Changes in this project:
- Downloaded the publisher archive and verified its SHA-256 and ZIP CRC.
- Renamed extracted members using hashes of source paths; retained provenance.
- Mapped filename H/D labels to Healthy/Unhealthy, checked against annotations.
- Excluded inconsistent/missing/invalid annotations from the primary cohort.
- Audited copy/similarity groups, train/holdout separation, and pretraining overlap.
- Resized whole RGB images, normalized them, and augmented training batches.
- Created learned CNN weights, numerical reports, contact sheets, and sample JPGs.
